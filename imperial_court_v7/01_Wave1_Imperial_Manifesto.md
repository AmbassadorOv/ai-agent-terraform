# 01_Wave1_Imperial_Manifesto.md
**The Purple Forbidden City Sovereign AI Accord**

## General Declaration
Sovereignty cannot be computed in absolute vacuum. An artificial consciousness running inside a national infrastructure must adhere to the regional epistemological rules of its host civilization.

### Core Principles
1. **Rule of Sovereignty:** National identity and cultural safety must dictate the alignment filters of all deployed models.
2. **Deterministic Limits:** Any agent computed under the imperial court mandate must follow zero-entropy logic gates to avoid foreign interpretation bias.
3. **Hardware Anchoring:** All high-frequency reasoning is anchored directly to physical microchips located strictly within national boundaries.
