## Deployment Steps
The initialization sequence runs across hierarchical clusters to spawn up to 100K software agents.

### Boot Sequence:
1. **Sanctification:** Bind system clock to Lunar constants (`molad.py`).
2. **Identification:** Poll for available workspaces and lock onto "China Sovereign AI Initiative".
3. **Bridge Verification:** Establish an encrypted TLS tunnel to Meta GPU tensors.
4. **Agent Spawning:** Distribute 1,000 active nodes (mDNS automatic discovery).
